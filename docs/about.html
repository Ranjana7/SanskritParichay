BhashaParichay

Mithu's Learning Universe

BhashaParichay is one magical learning world where children can explore Sanskrit, Spanish, and Portuguese adventures.

How children explore

Choose a language, open themed worlds, and play short activities that build listening, reading, speaking, and word-building confidence.

Children can replay activities for extra practice, and each language keeps its own progress journey.

Mithu's Reward Guide

  • ⭐ Stars: 1 star for each correct question, plus 5 bonus stars for each completed question group.
  • 💎 Gems: 1 gem per completed activity. Challenge of the Day gives 2 gems once per day when available.
  • 🌈 Unlock Worlds: Spend 50 available stars and 1 available gem to unlock the next world in order.
  • 🔁 Practice Again: Restart activities to try again and earn rewards on new successful attempts.
  • 🏅 Medals: Home medals celebrate real language-level completion, not just visiting or unlocking.
